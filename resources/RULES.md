# Welcome to the Miruka Server! 
*Please take a moment to read and understand our server rules to ensure a pleasant and respectful environment for everyone.*

# Server Rules

## 1. Don't be a Jerk
* The golden rule. Nobody likes trolls and obnoxious people.
* No spam, no advertisement, no NSFW content.
* Keep excessive controversial topics to yourself.
* We are not interested in drama you bring into here from other spaces.
* Be helpful. Point people into the right direction instead of talking down on them.
* Treat all members with respect and kindness.
* No harassment, bullying, or discrimination of any kind.
* Keep discussions civil and avoid personal attacks.

## 2. Language 
* Please keep chat and questions in English.
* This is an international community. English is the common ground people agreed on, not just in programming.
* You don't need to speak it perfectly, many of us aren't native speakers either! Just take your time and try your best!
* Vietnamese is the second supported language.
* Please use the appropriate language channels for your discussions.

## 3. Channel Usage
* Please ask questions in the relevant channels.
* Stick to one appropriate channel per question. Do not ask people to answer you in different channels.
* Use the appropriate channels for their intended purposes.
* Keep discussions relevant to the channel's topic.
* Avoid creating unnecessary channels or cluttering the server.

## 4. Mentions and Direct Messages 
* Do not randomly mention or direct message individual members.
* If you post your question in a public channel more people can see it and you will likely get your answer quicker.

## 5. Display Names
* Use readable display names.
* We understand that display names are a nice way to express yourself. Please consider other users viewing the channel, though!
* Your name should be easy to read.
* Your name should be easy to mention and refer to.
* Your name should not take up a lot of screen space.

## 6. Moderation and Enforcement 
* Follow the instructions of the moderators and staff.
* Do not argue with or disrespect moderator decisions.
* Rule violations may result in warnings, mutes, or bans.

## 7. Feedback and Suggestions
* We welcome your feedback and suggestions for improving the server and the game.
* Use the designated channels to provide constructive criticism and ideas.
