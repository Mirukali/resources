# Welcome to the Official Miruka Discord Server!
*To ensure a welcoming and enjoyable environment for everyone, please follow these rules*

# Server Rules for Miruka

# 1. General Conduct - Be Respectful!
* Be Respectful: Treat all members with respect. Hate speech, harassment, or any form of discrimination is not allowed.
* No Swearing: Avoid using profanity in any channel except for <#674958442645159967>.
* No Factions or Political Content: Do not create cliques or engage in seditious or political discussions.
* No Insults: Do not insult or demean others in the server.
* No Excessive Pings: Do not use `@everyone` or `@here` unless you are a server administrator.
* No NSFW Content: Do not post NSFW content (e.g., hentai, explicit material, loli) outside of designated NSFW channels.
* Respect Formatting: Avoid excessive use of CAPS LOCK to maintain readability.
* No Spamming: Do not send repetitive or similar messages in a short period.
* No Advertising: Refrain from posting promotional content in any channel.
* Stay On-Topic: Ensure discussions align with the purpose of the channel.

# 2. Bot Interaction - Play Fair!
* Fair Play Only: Exploiting bugs, using unauthorized tools, or farming with multiple accounts is prohibited.
* Command Usage: Use bot commands responsibly and only in designated channels.
* Feedback and Bug Reports: Report any bugs or provide suggestions in the appropriate channels. Constructive feedback is always welcome!

# 3. Community Participation - Join the Conversation!
* Respect Moderators: Follow instructions from moderators and staff members.
* Stay on Topic: Use the appropriate channels for discussions (e.g., support, general chat, or bot commands).
* No Self-Promotion: Promoting other servers, bots, or projects without permission is not allowed.


# 4. In-Game Economy and Rewards - Play Smart!
* Play Fairly: Avoid attempting to manipulate in-game currencies or rewards.
* Trading Rules: Trading items, currencies, or accounts is allowed but only for users who have reached a specific level. Trades are permitted exclusively in the support server. Guidelines for trading will be announced in the support server. Any dishonest intent will result in a ban.
* Donation Transparency: Donations or purchases related to Miruka are non-refundable.

# 5. Violations and Penalties - Stay Safe!
Rules violations will be handled as follows:
1. First Violation: Verbal warning.
2. Second Violation: Temporary timeout (duration determined by moderators).
3. Third Violation: Kick from the server.
4. Fourth Violation: Temporary ban for 7 days.
5. Fifth Violation: Permanent ban from the server.

Note: Severe violations (e.g., hate speech, scamming) may result in immediate action, skipping the earlier steps.

# 6. Language - Communicate Clearly!
* Please keep chat and questions in English.
* This is an international community. English is the common ground people agreed on.
* You don't need to speak it perfectly, many of us aren't native speakers either! Just take your time and try your best!
* Vietnamese is the second supported language.
* Please use the appropriate language channels for your discussions.

# 7. Channel Usage - Find Your Place!
* Please ask questions in the relevant channels.
* Stick to one appropriate channel per question. Do not ask people to answer you in different channels.
* If you are new to technical questions and the server, check out %GET_HELP_CHANNEL%!
* Use the appropriate channels for their intended purposes.
* Keep discussions relevant to the channel's topic.
* Avoid creating unnecessary channels or cluttering the server.


# 8. Mentions and Direct Messages - Respect Boundaries!
* Do not randomly mention or direct message individual members.
* If you post your question in a public channel more people can see it and you will likely get your answer quicker.

# 9. Display Names - Be Readable!
* Use readable display names.
* We understand that display names are a nice way to express yourself. Please consider other users viewing the channel, though!
* Your name should be easy to read.
* Your name should be easy to mention and refer to.
* Your name should not take up a lot of screen space.

# Scope of Application - Know the Rules!
* The general rules apply across all channels of the server.
* These rules take effect starting December 1, 2024.
* In addition to the general rules, specific channels may have their own rules. Members are encouraged to read the introduction and pinned messages of each channel before participating.
* Members who wish to contribute, supplement, or amend the rules can share their opinions in the <#1305838256230764624> channel.
* Rules may be supplemented or changed as needed. Updates will be announced widely, and members are responsible for staying informed. Any changes will be announced in the <#1290746146221723680> channel.
